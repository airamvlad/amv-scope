AMVScope - instalacija
==========================

WINDOWS (instaler)
1. Zatvori Bitwig.
2. Pokreni "AMVScope-x.y.z-Windows-Setup.exe".
   Windows moze da prikaze plavi prozor "Windows protected your PC", jer
   instaler nije potpisan. Klikni "More info", pa "Run anyway".
3. Otvori Bitwig. Plugin se zove "AMVScope".

MAC (instaler)
1. Zatvori Bitwig.
2. Desni klik na "AMVScope-x.y.z-macOS.pkg", pa "Open".
   Ako macOS ne dozvoli: System Settings > Privacy & Security, pa dole
   klikni "Open Anyway". To je zato sto instaler nije potpisan Apple
   sertifikatom.
3. Otvori Bitwig. Plugin se zove "AMVScope".

RUCNA INSTALACIJA (ako instaler ne radi)
Windows: raspakuj "...-Windows-manual.zip" i kopiraj folder
   "AMVScope.vst3" u  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
Mac: raspakuj "...-macOS-manual.zip", kopiraj "AMVScope.vst3" u
   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3  i jednom pokreni u Terminalu:
   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"AMVScope.vst3"

Ako se plugin ne pojavi: Bitwig > Settings > Locations > Plug-in Locations,
proveri da je VST3 folder na listi i klikni Rescan.

DEINSTALACIJA
Windows: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > AMVScope > Uninstall, ili obrisi
   folder  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\AMVScope.vst3
Mac: obrisi  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/AMVScope.vst3
